About

Built by one guy. For the towns nobody else is mapping.

Road Trip Atlas was conceived and built by Michael Chaves out of New Hampshire. The whole project runs on coffee, gas money, and a stubborn belief that the best weekends in America happen in towns the algorithm forgot.

Free for towns, regional Chambers, tourism boards, and travelers to use, share, and contribute to. Currently mapping Maine, New Hampshire, and Vermont — more states coming.

Questions, partnerships, or you just wanna say hi? Email shotgun@roadtripatlas.com. Shotgun handles all the messages — safety first. You hear that, kids? Never bother the driver. 😊
